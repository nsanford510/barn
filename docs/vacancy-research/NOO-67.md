## Finding

Oakland's vacant-property-tax determination for calendar year 2023 was challenged by the owner and upheld on judicial review in 2026; the court later said it would enter judgment after receiving no objections. The reproduced ruling says the City sent notice in March and May 2024 for the 2023 tax year, the owner appealed, and the hearing officer affirmed after a July 2024 administrative hearing. The court record says City evidence included blight/disrepair and no associated business license; the owner's claim of storage did not establish qualifying use or the required occupied storage area. The City acknowledged the property might qualify for a 2024-and-later exemption if a redevelopment planning permit was in place. A residential redevelopment application was reported in 2024, but that is not evidence of permit issuance, construction, or occupancy. The record supports the City's vacancy determination for tax year 2023, but does not establish when vacancy began or whether the property remains vacant today.
