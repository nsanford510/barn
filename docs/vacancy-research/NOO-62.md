# NOO-62 — 5TH ST, Oakland (APN 004 004900900)

## Finding

**Verdict:** likely_vacant  
**Confidence:** 0.76  
**Vacancy start / duration:** Unknown. The parcel is part of an undeveloped redevelopment site, but public evidence does not establish when it became unused or whether the land has ever been continuously unused.

The exact APN is shown in City planning records as part of the 500 Kirkham redevelopment site. A 2025 broker listing groups it with three other parcels in an offering described as commercial land, a redevelopment project, and a high-vacancy property. Those sources support the conclusion that no occupied dwelling or building is evidenced on the parcel. They do not prove parcel-specific physical conditions.

A June 2026 report says permits were filed for interim parking at 500 Kirkham. A filing does not establish that the permit was approved or that parking is operating, but it cautions against describing the parcel as continuously unused. Therefore the duration is unknown.

## Evidence

- [LoopNet / Colliers 500 Kirkham listing](https://www.loopnet.com/Listing/500-Kirkham-St-Oakland-CA/38139689/) — listing dated October 20, 2025; includes the exact APN in a four-parcel land offering marked high-vacancy and redevelopment. Parcel use is not separately described.
- [San Francisco YIMBY, June 26, 2026](https://sfyimby.com/2026/06/temporary-parking-proposed-for-500-kirkham-street-west-oakland.html) — reports permits filed for interim parking; proposal is not proof of approval or operation.
- [City of Oakland project agreements](https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Major-Development-Projects/Public-Private-Development-Project-Agreements) — identifies the site as a planned housing and retail development.

## Limits

The listing and planning materials concern a multi-parcel assemblage. No parcel-level occupancy record, executed parking permit, utility status, dated parcel-specific imagery, or verified vacancy-tax filing was found. No start date or continuous vacancy duration is assigned.
