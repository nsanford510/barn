## Rationale

It calls it a former service station. A community history reports that the building was demolished on January 29, 2015 for environmental cleanup. Oakland's February 2026 planning attachment independently matches the parcel address and APN. Together these support a vacant parcel, not an occupied dwelling. The evidence does not prove that the site remained vacant without interruption after 2016.
