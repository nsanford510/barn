- **Vacancy start / duration:** The latest dated public vacancy statement found is a commercial listing updated 2026-05-28. ## Rationale

The commercial marketing for this exact SRO property says “Property Delivered Vacant”; Oakland’s 2019 staff report also recorded 0 of 24 units occupied. These dated endpoints support likely vacancy, but do not establish uninterrupted vacancy, and later reuse or occupancy is unverified.

## Evidence

1. **2026-05-28 — listing reports vacant delivery.** PropertyShark’s listing for 2327 San Pablo Avenue identifies the property as a 23-unit multifamily building and states “Property Delivered Vacant.” Its page gives a last-updated date of 2026-05-28. This is broker marketing, not an independent occupancy inspection. [PropertyShark listing](https://www.propertyshark.com/cre/commercial-property/us/ca/oakland/2327-san-pablo-avenue/)
2. **2019-06-03 — no units occupied.** Oakland’s City Administrator report describes the 24-unit property as a vacant SRO and states “Currently, 0 of the 24 units are occupied.” This is a dated observation, not proof that vacancy continued after 2019. [Oakland agenda report (PDF)](https://oakland.legistar.com/gateway.aspx?ID=61f5fdd7-efe9-462c-8753-bcb54d89bcff.pdf&M=F)
3. **2026 listing record — status may have changed.** Crexi’s page for APN 3-21-6 repeats “Property Delivered Vacant” but indicates the listing was removed. That weakens any inference about occupancy after the marketing period; removal alone does not show a tenant moved in. [Crexi listing](https://www.crexi.com/properties/765077/california-2327-san-pablo-ave-oakland-ca-94612)

## Limits

The public evidence supports vacancy at two separated points (June 2019 and the 2026 marketing period), not a continuous vacancy interval. The 2019 City plan to acquire the property for transitional housing did not close, so that proposal is not evidence that the City housed residents there. No reliable public source found establishes the exact move-out date or occupancy after the 2026 listing was removed. Treat “about 4.5 months” only as a conditional minimum from the listing’s May 2026 statement, not a verified continuous duration.
