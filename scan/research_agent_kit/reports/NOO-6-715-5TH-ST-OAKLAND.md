## Assessment

The evidence supports a vacancy hypothesis but does not establish the parcel's current occupancy. The DTSC materials include this APN in a multi-parcel cleanup site, but the 2012 operating-facility closure refers to 715 4th St. A third-party parcel report classifies 715 5th as vacant commercial land, though it allows for miscellaneous improvements and is not a current inspection. **Verdict: unknown.
