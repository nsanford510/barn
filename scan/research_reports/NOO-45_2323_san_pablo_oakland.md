## Rationale

Public records identify an approved 16-unit project here, while a 2025 listing
for the combined 2321–2323 site inconsistently describes it as 100% leased and
development-ready. It spans parcels and does not establish occupancy at this
APN. No reliable vacancy start or duration is evidenced.

## Evidence

1. [Oakland Planning Commission report, June 1, 2022](https://cao-94612.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/SIGNED_2022.06.01-PC-Staff-Report_2021-APR_wAttachments_052522.pdf)
   lists 2323 San Pablo Ave, case PLN19233, as an entitled 16-unit project:
   one very-low-income and 15 market-rate units. Entitlement does not prove
   construction or occupancy.
2. [Colliers marketing flyer](https://s3.amazonaws.com/realnex.production/ListingAttachment/836043/13243564.pdf)
   (copyright 2022) describes 2323 San Pablo Ave as a fully entitled 16-unit
   condo development site with ground-floor commercial space. This is
   historical marketing, not evidence that the site was vacant or is vacant
   now.
3. [LoopNet listing](https://www.loopnet.com/Listing/2321-2323-San-Pablo-Ave-Oakland-CA/38424343/)
   has a November 12, 2025 market date for the combined 2321–2323 address.
   Search-indexed text says “100% leased” while also describing the property as
   ready for immediate development. The page is no longer advertised, and its
   parcel facts identify APN `003-0021-008-00`, not the target APN
   `003-0021-007-00`. The listing is ambiguous and cannot establish occupancy
   at the target parcel.

## Assessment

The sources are insufficient to determine current parcel-specific occupancy.
No parcel-specific utility status, current code/blight vacancy evidence, or
dated imagery establishing vacancy was located. The evidence also does not
support a start date or duration. An `unknown` verdict is appropriate.
