## Limits

The VPT bill is official county portal evidence of an assessed charge, not a direct observation of the property or a final adjudication that the property was physically empty. Oakland's process allows owners to petition or claim an exemption. The tax criterion supports fewer than 50 days in use during calendar year 2025; it does not identify the dates of use, prove a single continuous vacancy spell, or establish current 2026 occupancy. No exact vacancy start date is supported.
