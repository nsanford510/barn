## Finding

Public records show Kraft Automotive at this address in Oakland's FY 2017–18,
FY 2019–20, FY 2020–21, and FY 2021–22 Municipal Regional Stormwater Permit
annual reports. The FY 2021–22 report classifies it as auto repair. This is
evidence of a business associated with the property through that reporting
period, not proof that the business operated continuously or that the property
is occupied today.

Cushman & Wakefield currently markets the 4,469 sq ft building and all of its
available space for sale. The listing is consistent with vacancy but does not
state that the building is vacant; commercial property can be marketed while
occupied. An undated historic flyer likewise offered the site for lease or sale
and disclosed that its information had not been verified.

The dated occupancy evidence and current listing conflict or leave a substantial
time gap. No reliable public record reviewed here establishes a vacancy date,
so vacancy duration is unknown. The appropriate queue verdict is `unknown`.
