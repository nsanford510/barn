# Vacancy research: 578 7th St, Oakland

**APN:** 001-0209-015-00 (also shown as 001 020901500)  
**Research checked:** 2026-10-15  
**Verdict:** `unknown` (confidence 0.57)  
**Vacancy start / duration:** Vacancy is documented by September 2021 and again in a January 2025 listing. This establishes at least about 3 years 4 months between public observations, not continuous vacancy. Start date and duration through the present are unverified.

## Finding

The parcel was described as a vacant one-story commercial building in 2021, and a January 2025 commercial listing still described its current use as a vacant restaurant and market. That makes vacancy plausible, but the latest direct vacancy statement located is dated January 2025. A proposed 57-unit redevelopment remained in planning records, but plans and renderings do not establish construction, completion, or occupancy. No sufficiently current occupancy, utility, or permit-closeout evidence was found to classify the parcel's status in October 2026.

**Rationale for queue:** Public reporting documented a vacant former commercial building in 2021; a Jan 2025 listing still calls the existing restaurant/market vacant. A 57-unit redevelopment was proposed, but no source located confirms it was built or occupied. The evidence supports vacancy through Jan 2025, not continuous vacancy or present occupancy in Oct 2026; status and total duration remain unknown.

## Evidence

1. **Vacant use stated in a commercial property listing (2025-01-17).** LoopNet identifies the property and APN, calls it a vacant restaurant and market, and describes the extant one-story, 5,982-square-foot building. Its listing date is January 17, 2025; the page now says it is no longer advertised. This is direct listing evidence of vacancy as of the listing, not proof of status after it left the market. [LoopNet listing](https://www.loopnet.com/Listing/578-7th-St-Oakland-CA/34514940/)
2. **Vacant building reported by September 2021.** San Francisco YIMBY's September 2021 account of the proposed redevelopment says the existing single-story commercial structure was vacant and would be demolished. This is a dated secondary report, not a city inspection. [SF YIMBY, Sep. 2021](https://sfyimby.com/2021/09/development-permit-filed-for-578-7th-street-downtown-oakland.html)
3. **City planning record confirms proposal, not completion.** Oakland's 2021 sites inventory lists PLN21175 at this address/APN as “Approved-Pending Appeal,” with 57 units and expected completion 2025-12-31. The inventory is project pipeline data; it does not show that the building permit was completed or residents moved in. [Oakland Appendix C: Sites Inventory](https://cao-94612.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/Appendix-C-Sites-Inventory_clean.pdf)
4. **Architect's project page describes planned housing.** The project page describes 55 functional homes and shows design imagery. It does not provide a completion date or evidence of occupancy, so it cannot resolve current vacancy. [Stanley Saitowitz / Natoma Architects](https://www.saitowitz.com/578-7th-street)

Treat the observed span (September 2021 to January 2025) as separated observations only. Do not report it as a continuous vacancy period. Recheck Oakland permit/occupancy records or obtain a current street-level observation before upgrading the verdict.
